# drownman
a game like the classic hangman but the man drowns instead of hanging.

## origin of the idea
i had this idea in my mind since the (very very) early days of my coding when i didnt even know html tags. my sister had watched a video on youtube that made a joke on letting others choose your life, how funny it would be if people played a game of hangman to decide whether to drown a man or let him live, this gave her (yes, her) an idea for this kind of a game. she didnt even start building it so i stole her idea for this game. and now im gonna get stuff for free without even having to give credit to my sis for the idea (evil laugh)

## features
- a keypad to guess the letter and the letters which are already guessed
- when letter guessed its bg color change
- total number of incorrect guesses
- waves of water coming up after every incorrect guess eventually covering the whole screen and drowning you
